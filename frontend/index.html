Conformal & Abstention Architecture

Conformal Prediction Set C(X)

Constructs sets {y : s(x, y) ≤ q̂} where nonconformity score s = 1 - p̂_y.

• {0}: Confidently Legitimate
• {1}: Confidently Fraud
• {0, 1}: Uncertain / Ambiguous
• ∅: Out-of-Distribution Anomaly
Selective Decision Rule
ALLOW: C(X) = {0} AND P(Fraud) ≤ τ_accept
BLOCK: C(X) = {1} AND P(Fraud) ≥ τ_block
ABSTAIN: C(X) ∈ {{0, 1}, ∅} OR intermediate score

Abstained payments route directly to Human Review or Step-Up Challenge (e.g. 3DS / Biometrics) and are never blindly accepted.

Finite-Sample Quantile Formula
q̂_α = Quantile_((⌈(n+1)(1-α)⌉ / n))({s_1, ..., s_n})

Theoretical Guarantee & Exchangeability Disclaimer

Conformal coverage guarantees P(Y ∈ C(X)) ≥ 1 - α and risk-controlled false-negative bounds hold strictly under the assumption that calibration samples and test samples are exchangeable (identically and independently distributed). In real-world adversarial payment environments, zero-day evasion, distribution shift, and concept drift may violate exchangeability. FraudForge AI therefore utilizes conformal abstention alongside continuous adversarial red-teaming to actively detect when distribution shift occurs.
